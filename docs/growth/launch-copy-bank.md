### FAQ

**Does Memephant connect to my AI accounts?**
No. The core workflow is copy/paste. You choose what to send.

**Does it store my data in the cloud?**
Core use is local-first. Optional cloud backup is opt-in.

**Which AI tools does it support?**
The app supports platform-specific handoffs for major chat and coding tools, including ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Codex, Grok, and local models.

**Is this a prompt manager?**
Not exactly. It is project memory: summary, goals, decisions, current state, rules, files, and handoff context.

**Is it free?**
Memephant is free during early access.

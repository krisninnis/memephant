FAQ

Claude to ChatGPT handoff questions

Why do I lose context when I move from Claude to ChatGPT?

Each AI tool keeps its own separate memory of a conversation. When you open ChatGPT after working in Claude, it has no record of your stack, goals, or earlier decisions, so you have to re-explain everything from scratch.

How does Memephant handle a Claude to ChatGPT handoff?

You store your project once in Memephant. When you switch tools, you pick ChatGPT, copy a handoff shaped for it, and paste it at the start of a new chat. The same works in reverse, from ChatGPT back to Claude.

Does Memephant send my project to Claude or ChatGPT automatically?

No. Memephant does not connect to hosted AI platforms for core handoffs. It formats your context for you to copy and paste yourself, and you review every handoff before sharing it.

Is Memephant free?

Yes. Memephant is free during early access and needs no account to create projects or generate handoffs.

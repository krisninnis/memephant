Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Memephant is local-first project continuity software. By default, project memory is stored on your device and nothing is sent to an AI service automatically.

What Memephant Stores

Memephant stores project names, summaries, goals, decisions, notes, linked-folder metadata, checkpoints, settings, and other project memory you choose to enter. Desktop project data is stored in the operating system application data directory.

When Data Leaves Your Device

Data leaves your device only when you choose a feature that requires it, such as:

  • copying an export to your clipboard and pasting it into another service;
  • signing in and enabling optional cloud backup;
  • using hosted account, checkout, billing, or support flows;
  • enabling optional crash reporting.

Telemetry and Crash Reporting

Memephant does not use product analytics or ad tracking in the core app. Crash reporting is optional, disabled by default, and intended to send error diagnostics rather than project content. Do not enable it if you do not want diagnostic data sent to a third-party crash reporting provider.

Local AI

Private Mode is optional. When enabled, Memephant connects to your configured local Ollama endpoint on this device. Local AI is not required for normal use.

Cloud Backup

Cloud backup is optional and requires sign-in. If enabled, project data is transmitted over HTTPS to the configured backend so it can sync across your devices.

Your Control

  • You can use Memephant without signing in.
  • You can download your data from the app.
  • You can clear local project data from the app.
  • You can disconnect cloud backup if you enabled it.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to hello@memephant.com.
